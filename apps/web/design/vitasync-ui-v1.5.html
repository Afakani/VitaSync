Before you start

VitaSync is a university prototype and not a medical device.

You are talking to an AI, not a doctor. VitaSync gives general health information. It does not make diagnoses and does not assess your values or your situation.

In an emergency, call 112. For non-urgent medical help outside office hours, call 116117.

Your messages are processed on a project computer. No transfer to third parties or outside the EU. Chats are deleted automatically after 30 days. Please use test data only, no real health data.

You can withdraw your consent at any time in the settings and export or delete your data.
